ABOUT BULLION BRIDGE

INTERNATIONAL METALS.

International metals logistics, sourcing and consultancy.

Bullion Bridge Limited works across the physical metals supply chain, including gold, silver, copper and cobalt. The business connects suppliers and buyers and supports sourcing, logistics and technical expertise.

The company describes its work with community-based producers and aims to support local infrastructure and economic development.

Its activities include precious metals trading, production financing and mining.
